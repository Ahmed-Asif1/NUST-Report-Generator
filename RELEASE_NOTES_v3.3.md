# NUST Report Generator — Beta v3.3

### 🚀 What's New in v3.3
- **Favicon & Web App Manifest**: Full RealFaviconGenerator suite integrated into the web GUI. Crisp high-resolution tab icon, Android PWA icons, and embedded executable branding.
- **NBS & Humanities Expansion**: Added dedicated course profiles and domain answer blocks for NUST Business School (Marketing, Finance, Accounting, SWOT Analysis) and Humanities (Islamiyat Hadith/Ayah citations, Pakistan Studies).
- **EE & CE Engineering Enhancements**: Dedicated support for Electrical Engineering (DSO oscilloscope waveforms, circuit schematics, simulation) and Computer Engineering.
- **Task Search & Duplicate Text Fix**: Resolved duplicate task text rendering on instruction toggles and enabled fuzzy block searching in the GUI.
- **Hardware-Bound Security**: Cryptographic activation flow protecting distribution while keeping the software 100% offline.

---

### 📦 Installation & Setup
1. Download **`NUST_Report_Generator_Beta_v3.3.zip`** from the assets below.
2. Extract the `.zip` archive to a folder on your computer.
3. Run **`NUSTReportGenerator.exe`**.
4. Enter the one-time **Activation Password** provided by your distributor/admin.
5. Your browser will open the generator interface (`http://127.0.0.1:8765`).
